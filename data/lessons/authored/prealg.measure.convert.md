# Customary unit conversion

A measurement can be written in different units without changing its size. Converting between units multiplies by a conversion factor, which is a ratio equal to 1.

## Use a conversion factor

The factor relates two units, such as 12 inches to 1 foot. Write it as a fraction:

\[
\frac{12 \text{ inches}}{1 \text{ foot}} = 1.
\]

Multiplying by 1 does not change the amount, only the unit. Choose the form that cancels the unit you have and leaves the unit you want.

## Multiply or divide

To convert a larger unit to a smaller unit, multiply. For 2.5 feet,

\[
2.5 \times 12 = 30,
\]

so 2.5 feet is 30 inches. To convert a smaller unit to a larger unit, divide by the same factor. The factor tells you which operation, and the unit tells you which direction.

## Check the size

A length in inches should be a larger number than the same length in feet, because an inch is smaller than a foot. For 2.5 feet and 30 inches, the number grew as the unit shrank. If the answer moves the wrong way, the conversion factor was used upside down.
