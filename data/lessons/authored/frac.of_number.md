# A fraction of a whole number

## Multiplying by the fraction

A fraction of a number is the fraction multiplied by the number. Two thirds of twelve is two thirds times twelve, which is eight.

The wording "of" is the signal for multiplication, and it is worth reading it that way rather than as a new operation.

## Why the answer is whole here

When the denominator divides the whole number evenly, the answer is a whole number and no fraction remains. Twelve splits into three groups of four, and taking two of those groups gives eight.

When the denominator does not divide the number, the answer stays a fraction, and the same multiplication gives it: two thirds of ten is twenty thirds, which is a little over six.

## The same as sharing

Reading the calculation as sharing is what makes it concrete: split the whole into as many equal parts as the denominator, then take as many of them as the numerator. That picture is also the reason the word "of" means multiply, since the two descriptions give the same number.

The two readings, multiply, or share and take, are the arithmetic and the model, and a learner who has both can check one against the other.
