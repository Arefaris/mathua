# A fraction of a fraction

## Multiplying twice

A fraction of a fraction is a multiplication: half of three quarters is one half times three quarters, which is three eighths. The word "of" means multiply here for the same reason it does with whole numbers.

The answer is smaller than either fraction, and that is the check worth making. A fraction of a fraction takes part of a part, so it cannot exceed what it started from.

## Why the denominators multiply

Splitting each quarter in half produces eighths, so the denominators multiply. The numerators multiply for the same reason applied to the parts being taken, which is why the rule is symmetric in the two fractions.

Reading the calculation as splitting and taking is what makes the rule memorable rather than arbitrary.

## Where it appears

Scaling a recipe does this twice: taking three quarters of a quantity and then half of that is a fraction of a fraction, and the answer is the product. The same computation appears whenever a proportion is applied to a proportion, which is why the operation is worth having fluently.

The result is also the reason probabilities multiply when both events must happen, since taking a fraction of a fraction and taking a fraction of a population are the same arithmetic.
