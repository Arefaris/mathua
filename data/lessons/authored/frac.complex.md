# Complex fractions

## A fraction over a fraction

A complex fraction has a fraction in its numerator, its denominator, or both. It is not a new kind of number: it is one fraction divided by another, and the long bar is the division sign.

Reading the bar as division is the step that turns the expression into something the four operations already handle.

## Multiplying by the reciprocal

Dividing by a fraction means multiplying by its reciprocal, so a fraction over a fraction becomes a product. The two denominators move, and the result is one fraction.

That is the fastest route and the one to use, rather than finding a common denominator for a problem that is really a multiplication.

## Simplifying the result

The product is usually not in lowest terms, so the result is reduced as the last step. Cancelling before multiplying is equivalent and often quicker, since the factors are visible before they are multiplied out.

The answer is the same either way, and the choice between them is a matter of how large the numbers are rather than of correctness.
