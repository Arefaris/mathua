# Dividing mixed numbers

## Converting first

Dividing a mixed number by a fraction starts by turning the mixed number into an improper fraction: the whole number times the denominator plus the numerator, over the denominator. For two and a half that is five halves.

The conversion is the step that turns a mixed number into the form the division rules are written for, and skipping it is the usual error.

## Multiplying by the reciprocal

Once both numbers are fractions, dividing means multiplying by the reciprocal. The mixed number's conversion and the reciprocal are two steps, and they can be done in either order, but both are needed.

The answer is a fraction, and it can be converted back to a mixed number if that is the form the problem is in. Keeping it as a fraction is usually safer, since converting back introduces another chance for an arithmetic slip.

## Checking the size

Dividing by a fraction smaller than one makes the result larger, and the reciprocal is larger than one. So a mixed number divided by a proper fraction must exceed the mixed number, and an answer smaller than it means the reciprocal was not applied.
