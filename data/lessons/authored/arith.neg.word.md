# Integer word problems

A word problem about temperature or elevation starts at a signed value. The words that describe the change decide whether to add a positive or a negative number.

## Choose the sign of the change

A rise, an increase or a gain is positive. A fall, a drop or a descent is negative. "The temperature fell 8 degrees" means a change of \(-8\), even though the sentence contains no minus sign.

Write the starting value and the signed change before calculating. For a temperature of \(-4\) degrees that rises 9 degrees, the starting value is \(-4\) and the change is \(+9\).

## Add the signed change

Add the starting value and the change:

\[
-4 + 9 = 5.
\]

The temperature is now 5 degrees. Adding a negative change works the same way: \(-4 + (-9) = -13\). On a number line, a positive change moves right and a negative change moves left.

## Check the direction

Ask whether the result makes sense. A rise from a negative temperature can stay below zero or cross above it. A fall from a positive temperature can do the same in the other direction. If the result moves opposite to the stated direction, the sign of the change was reversed.

For a diver at \(-12\) meters who rises 5 meters, the change is \(+5\) and the new position is \(-7\) meters. The diver is still below the surface, which fits a rise of only 5 meters.
