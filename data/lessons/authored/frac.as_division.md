# A fraction as a division

## The bar is a division

The fraction bar and the division sign mean the same thing: three quarters is three divided by four. That is not a coincidence of notation but the definition, which is why a fraction can be read as a division and a division of whole numbers as a fraction.

Reading it that way is what makes the answer to a sharing problem a fraction rather than a decimal.

## Sharing equally

Cutting a length of three into four equal pieces gives a piece of three quarters, and the piece is smaller than the original because it is one of four parts. The arithmetic is one division, and writing it as a fraction keeps the answer exact.

The same calculation in decimals would be 0.75, which is the same number written differently. A fraction is preferred when the division does not terminate, since it stays exact.

## Why the two agree

The division of a whole number by another can always be written as a fraction with the dividend on top and the divisor below, and the value is unchanged by the rewrite. That is the definition of what a fraction is, applied in the direction that turns an operation into a number.

The connection is worth stating because it explains two things at once: why a fraction can appear as the answer to a division, and why dividing by a fraction is a multiplication.
