# Resultant Vectors

**Resultant:** The single vector that has the same effect as two or more vectors added together. For perpendicular components $a$ and $b$, its magnitude is $\sqrt{a^2 + b^2}$ and its direction is $\arctan(b/a)$ from the horizontal.

## Adding perpendicular components

Perpendicular components do not interfere: the horizontal part of the resultant is the sum of the horizontal parts, and the vertical part is the sum of the vertical parts. Pythagoras then gives the magnitude, and the inverse tangent gives the angle.

## Worked: the 3-4-5 resultant

1. A vector has perpendicular components 3 and 4.
2. The magnitude is $\sqrt{9 + 16} = 5$.
3. The direction is $\arctan(4/3) \approx 53.1^\circ$ above the horizontal.

## Components in general

For vectors at arbitrary angles, resolve each into horizontal and vertical components first, add the components separately, then recompute magnitude and direction. The same procedure handles forces, velocities and displacements. The resultant is zero exactly when the component sums are both zero, which is the condition for equilibrium.
